TenderPilot is installed.

HOW TO START
Open the Start menu and click TenderPilot. A small black window opens and your browser shows TenderPilot.
Keep that window open while you work. Close it to stop TenderPilot.

The first time, create your organization and an administrator account (password of at least 10 characters).

DEMO VERSION
"TenderPilot Demo" is a separate practice copy filled with made-up (SYNTHETIC) data. Its sign-in details are shown
on its sign-in page. Nothing you do in the demo touches your real data.

WHERE YOUR DATA IS KEPT
Real data:  %LOCALAPPDATA%\TenderPilot
Demo data:  %LOCALAPPDATA%\TenderPilot-Demo
Uninstalling or reinstalling TenderPilot does NOT delete these folders.

BACKUPS
Start menu > TenderPilot > "Back up TenderPilot data" writes a zip into %LOCALAPPDATA%\TenderPilot\backups.
Copy it to a pen drive or another computer every week.

INCLUDED
Scanned-tender reading (Tesseract OCR, English and Hindi) is built in.

OPTIONAL
Old Word/Excel files (.doc, .xls) need LibreOffice (free, www.libreoffice.org). Without it, such files are marked
"needs conversion" and everything else still works. Settings & audit > System checks shows what is installed.

TenderPilot never submits bids, signs, pays or creates certificates. It runs only on this computer (127.0.0.1).
